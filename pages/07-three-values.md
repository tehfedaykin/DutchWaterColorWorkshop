## Light · Midtone · Dark

<div class="dw-values">
  <div class="dw-value-block" style="background:#f4ecd8; color:#2b2013;">Light</div>
  <div class="dw-value-block" style="background:#c99a2e; color:#2b2013;">Midtone</div>
  <div class="dw-value-block" style="background:#4a2f1a; color:#f4ecd8;">Dark</div>
</div>

<div class="dw-card">
Here's a nice bit of freedom: Vermeer paintings work because they
commit to just <strong>three values</strong>, not ten. You don't need
to solve for every shade — just decide what's light, what's mid,
and what's dark, and the rest follows.
</div>
