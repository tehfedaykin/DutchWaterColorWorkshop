## Vermeer never touched watercolor

<div text-sm opacity-80>
His paint was oil-based — pigment ground into linseed oil and built
up in slow, thin layers called glazes.
</div>

<div grid grid-cols-2 gap-4 mt-4 text-sm text-left>
<div class="dw-card">

**His medium: oil**
- Opaque — covers what's beneath it
- Dries over days or weeks
- Highlights get painted in with white

</div>
<div class="dw-card">

**Our medium: watercolor**
- Transparent — light passes through it
- Dries in minutes
- Highlights are just paper left alone

</div>
</div>

<div text-xs opacity-60 mt-3>
Same families of pigment, though — his ultramarine, ground from lapis
lazuli, once cost more than gold.
</div>
