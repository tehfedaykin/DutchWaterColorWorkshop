<div class="dw-eyebrow">The Vermeer Trick</div>

## One window, doing all the work

<div class="dw-card">
Look at almost any Vermeer interior and you'll notice the same
thing: light drifts in from <strong>one side</strong>, usually the
upper left, and everything else in the painting is just describing
how it falls away from there.
</div>

<div class="dw-card">
Try it yourself today: set your still life by <strong>one window or
one lamp</strong>, and leave it at that. No fill light, no second
lamp fighting it. One source, and suddenly everything feels intentional.
</div>
