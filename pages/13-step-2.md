## A big, wet wash for the background

<div class="dw-card">
Water first, then a little pigment — <strong>ultramarine + a touch
of burnt sienna</strong>. Tilt the paper and let it wander. This
becomes the mood of the whole piece.
</div>

<div class="dw-card">
If it feels like it's getting away from you, it's almost always
just <strong>too much water</strong> — ease off the pigment, breathe,
it dries lighter than it looks.
</div>
