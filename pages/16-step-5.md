## Now the fun part: stopping

<div class="dw-card" style="font-size:1.3rem">
The white of the paper <strong>is</strong> your highlight — it's
already doing its job. Resist the urge to add a sixth color or
fuss over one more detail. Let it be done.
</div>

<div class="dw-card">
That's the whole Dutch Masters trick, start to finish: <strong>a few
colors, strong light, and the confidence to leave the whites alone.</strong>
</div>
