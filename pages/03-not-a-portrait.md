## This is not <em>Girl with a Pearl Earring</em> — and that's the fun part

<div class="dw-card">
Vermeer spent months on a single painting. We get 50 minutes. So
instead of chasing a masterpiece, we're chasing one small, satisfying
feeling: what a lemon looks like sitting in soft window light.
< br/>
That's why the subject is simple — a piece of fruit, a folded cloth,
a moody little mug. Less to draw means more time to actually notice
how light behaves.
</div>
