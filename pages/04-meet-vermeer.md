# Johannes Vermeer

<div class="dw-card">

**Delft, 1632–1675**

A Dutch Golden Age painter who worked slowly, in one room, with
one window — and turned that quiet constraint into something
unmistakably his own.

</div>

<div class="dw-card">

**Why we're borrowing from him**

Vermeer didn't need a huge palette or a dramatic scene to make
something beautiful. He needed **good light and a little patience**
— which happens to be exactly what today is about.

</div>
