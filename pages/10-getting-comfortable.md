## Get comfortable with the water first

<div class="dw-card">
Watercolor rewards a light touch and a little trust. Load your
brush with water, then just a touch of pigment — you can always
add more, but you can never take it back out.
</div>

<div class="dw-card">
Before touching your real paper, play on your scrap sheet: one wet
wash, one crisp dry-brush line, one color dropped into another
while it's still wet. There's no wrong answer here — you're just
learning how the water wants to move.
</div>
